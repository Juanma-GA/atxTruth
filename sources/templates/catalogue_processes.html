Support element production

Our support element production turns engineering, LSA/PSA and customer data into compliant technical publications and training deliverables, through proven work processes aligned with S1000D, S2000M, S6000T, DITA and ATA iSpec 2200. With strong expertise in CSDB/CCMS platforms (Xignal, ADAM, Eagle, Hico…), XML editors (PTC Arbortext, Oxygen, XMetaL…) and custom tool development and integration, ALTEN is a trusted, low-risk partner able to manage the entire production scope.

ISS and obsolescence

Our ISS process connects customer, engineering and ILS/IPS teams to solve in-service issues, manage obsolescence and continuously optimise support (ISSO). With strong experience in managing ISS requests and as part of engineering design & development (D&D) departments, ALTEN manages the interfaces between design teams, ILS/IPS teams and customers, ensuring smooth execution of PHST, spares flow, obsolescence and technical issue resolution.
